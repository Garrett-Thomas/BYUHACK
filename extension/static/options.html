Warmline capture

Warmline links each company to its LinkedIn company page once. Open a company from Warmline with Find {company} on LinkedIn, click into the right company, and use the Save “…” as “…” button this extension adds at the bottom right of that page. After that, people on LinkedIn searches opened from Warmline and scoped to that company (1st-, 2nd- and 3rd-degree results) are saved. Nothing else you browse on LinkedIn is read or saved, and the extension never clicks, scrolls or pages through results for you.
